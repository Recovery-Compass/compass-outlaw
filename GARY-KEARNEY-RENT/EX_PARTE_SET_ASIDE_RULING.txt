MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

I. INTRODUCTION
This case presents a classic instance of "surprise" and "excusable neglect" warranting relief under CCP § 473(b). Defendants filed a timely Demurrer. The Court Clerk returned the conformed copy of the face page with a handwritten hearing date of February 19, 2026. Relying on this official Court instruction, Defendants prepared to appear on February 19.

However, the Court seemingly held the hearing on February 17, 2026—two days early—and overruled the Demurrer in Defendants' absence. This violates due process. A party cannot be penalized for obeying the written instruction of the Court Clerk.

II. RELIEF IS MANDATORY UNDER CCP § 473(b)
The Court "shall" vacate any judgment, dismissal, or other order resulting from mistake, inadvertence, surprise, or excusable neglect. Here, the "surprise" is objective and documented: The Clerk wrote "2/19/26" on the filing. It is legally impossible for a defendant to be expected to appear on a date different from the one written on their conformed copy.

III. CONCLUSION
For the foregoing reasons, Defendants respectfully request that the Court set aside the February 17, 2026 ruling and reset the demurrer hearing to allow for a hearing on the merits.
