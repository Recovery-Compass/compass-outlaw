## PROMPT 1: MEMORANDUM OF POINTS & AUTHORITIES
**Strength Impact**: +5 → Moves from theory to filed argument
**Priority**: 🔴 CRITICAL — Do first

```
Draft the Memorandum of Points and Authorities for the Ex Parte Application
to Set Aside the February 17, 2026 Order in Case No. 26PDUD00325
(Kearney v. Jones, et al., Pasadena Courthouse).

Use ONLY the validated elements from
Gary W Kearney Case Matter/EX-PARTE-SET-ASIDE/VALIDATED_ELEMENTS_Final_Draft_Matrix.md

Structure as follows:

I.   INTRODUCTION (½ page — what happened, what we're asking for)
II.  STATEMENT OF FACTS (1–2 pages — procedural chronology, clerk's date,
     no notice, Minute Order language, discovery on Feb 19, immediate action)
III. ARGUMENT
     A. PRIMARY: Discretionary Relief Under CCP § 473(b)
        - Lead with Rappleyea anti-forfeiture policy
        - "Surprise" from clerk's handwritten date
        - "Excusable neglect" from absence of notice
        - Proactively cite McClain v. Kissler — acknowledge mandatory prong
          is unavailable to pro per, pivot to discretionary
        - Timeliness (3 days after discovery, well within 6 months)
     B. ALTERNATIVE 1: Due Process Violation
        - Mullane — notice is constitutional minimum
        - Au-Yang BY ANALOGY — do NOT claim § 594(a) directly governs
          demurrers; argue the principle extends
        - CCP § 473(d) — statutory vehicle if void
        - Kabran framework — in the alternative, even if voidable, set aside
          under discretionary authority
     C. ALTERNATIVE 2: Inherent Equitable Authority
        - County of San Diego v. Gorham
        - Rappleyea three-part test (meritorious defense, valid reason, diligence)
     D. PREEMPTIVE REBUTTAL: Gov. Code § 68607
        - Calendar management ≠ due process override
        - Court's own Minute Order confirms advancement from 02/19
IV.  URGENCY / IRREPARABLE HARM (CRC 3.1202(c))
     - Answer deadline running from Feb 17 = risk of default
     - Loss of possession of residence
V.   CONCLUSION

Do NOT include:
- Mandatory CCP § 473(b) relief claims
- "VOID for lack of jurisdiction" as affirmative finding
- Duty of Candor / Heckmann accusations
- McKnight v. Faber citation
- LASC Local Rule 3.25
- Retaliatory eviction or illegal dwelling detail (save for Answer)

Format: Court-ready. 12pt Times New Roman equivalent. Clear headings.
Every case citation must include the full reporter cite and pinpoint page.
```
