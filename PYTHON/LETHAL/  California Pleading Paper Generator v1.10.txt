### 🔧 Usage Pattern

```python
from generate_ca_pleading_v1 import PleadingEngine, PleadingConfig

cfg = PleadingConfig()
cfg.filer_name = "NUHA SAYEGH"
cfg.court_line2 = "COUNTY OF LOS ANGELES – PASADENA COURTHOUSE"
cfg.case_number = "Case No.: 26PDUD00325"
cfg.footer_text = "DEMURRER TO UNLAWFUL DETAINER"
cfg.total_pages = 3

e = PleadingEngine(cfg)
e.begin_page()
e.draw_caption()
# ... build content with e.text(), e.text_wrapped(), e.rich_line() ...
e.save()
```
